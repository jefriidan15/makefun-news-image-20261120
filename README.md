A newspaper front page summing up the world news of **Friday 20 November
2026** in one image. The masthead names the day; four illustrated panels
cover the day's biggest stories, and a bottom strip carries four more.

- **Kyiv bridges hit** — Russian overnight strikes kill 8 and injure 28
  across nine regions; 2 more killed at a railway station in Lviv.
- **Poland airspace violated** — three objects, allegedly two missiles and
  a drone, cross into Poland overnight.
- **EU–UK summit in Brussels** — a youth mobility scheme is set to be
  agreed, letting up to 100,000 young people a year travel, work and study.
- **Florida floods** — the St. Johns River hits a record 18.7 ft, with
  about 100 rescue locations and 65 homes under mandatory evacuation in
  Palm Bay Estates.

Also today: Estonia closes its Russian border for 6 months; Iraq moves
against Kataib Hezbollah; Myanmar dismisses the ICJ genocide ruling; the UK
admits offensive cyber operations against Russia, China, Iran and North
Korea.

The image (`entry.png`, 1024×1024) was drawn programmatically with PIL —
no external images. `main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies
it to the output directory and verifies the copy byte for byte.

## Run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

The output is `out/entry.png`, byte-identical on every run.
